# Loop Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

## 1. Data Collection
Loop is designed to be privacy-first.
- We do not require students to create accounts to view events.
- We do not track your location.
- We do not sell any data to third parties.

## 2. Authentication
For campus club coordinators who submit events, we use Firebase Authentication. This collects your email address solely for the purpose of verifying your identity as an authorized club representative.

## 3. Usage Data
We collect anonymous crash reports using Sentry to help us fix bugs and improve the app's stability.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions, please contact the Loop student development team at IIT Delhi.
