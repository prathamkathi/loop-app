# Boards
* **Board for Hostel Management (BHM)**
  - `@bhmiitd`
  - Responsible for the management and maintenance of all student hostels and messes at IIT Delhi.
* **Board for Recreational and Creative Activities (BRCA)**
  - `@brcaiitd`
  - The apex body managing all cultural and co-curricular clubs, organizing fests and promoting student creativity.
* **Board for Sports Activities (BSA)**
  - `@bsa.iitd`
  - Manages all sports facilities, inter-hostel tournaments, and the institute's participation in Inter-IIT Sports Meets.
* **Board for Student Publications (BSP)**
  - `@bsp.iitdelhi`
  - The official student media and publication body, producing newsletters, magazines, and journalistic content for the campus.
* **Board for Student Welfare (BSW)**
  - `@bsw_iitd`
  - Focuses on student wellbeing, mentorship, financial assistance, and mental health support.
